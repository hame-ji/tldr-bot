Title: Communicating Expertise in Software Development

TL;DR: Senior developers often struggle to convey their expertise clearly, leading to mixed messages.

Key points:
- Senior developers focus on different priorities based on their role.
- One perspective highlights complexity avoidance, while another emphasizes stability.
- AI's rise challenges these approaches, urging a balance between speed and quality.

Evidence:
- Senior developers may avoid complexity to reduce risk.
- Business needs stability, which conflicts with rapid development.

Caveat:
- Lack of clear evidence on AI's impact or specific examples.
